1. DATASET OVERVIEW
Dataset: RDD-India v9
Total images: 7,706
Training images: 6,215

2. IMAGE QUALITY
All 7,706 images are readable.
All images have dimensions 416 x 416.
All images are stored in RGB mode.
No widespread extreme brightness or low-contrast issue was detected.

3. IMAGE-LABEL CONSISTENCY
No missing image-label pairs were found.
Empty label files: 2,623.
Empty labels may represent valid negative images or missing annotations.
They require further visual investigation.

4. ANNOTATION STRUCTURE
Total bounding boxes: 12,270.
Structurally valid annotations: 12,270.
Invalid annotations: 0.
All labels passed the checked YOLO format and coordinate-boundary rules.
Structural validity does not guarantee visual correctness.

D40 is the most frequent class.

6. BOUNDING-BOX ANALYSIS
Bounding-box area and aspect-ratio distributions were examined.
Some very small boxes and unusually narrow or wide boxes were found.
These are potential review cases, not automatically incorrect annotations.
D20 had 60.32 percent of its boxes touching an image edge.

11. IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- Visual correctness of all annotations has not been established.
- Empty labels may contain missed annotations.
- Six train-involved near-duplicate pairs remain uncertain.
- Validation-Test near-duplicate candidates still need review.
- Source location and recording-session metadata are unavailable.
- The audit does not establish that the dataset is completely free of leakage.

12. CONCLUSION
The dataset passed the automated structural checks performed so far.
However, visual annotation quality, possible location overlap,
and severity-label reliability require further investigation.

Original images and YOLO labels have been preserved.

13. VALIDATION-TEST NEAR-DUPLICATE MANUAL REVIEW

Total candidate pairs: 110
Manually reviewed: 10
Same location, different photographs: 8
Different locations: 2
Unreviewed candidates: 100

One reviewed pair had already been examined during the
earlier pHash threshold-5 review.

The eight same-location findings indicate possible
location overlap between validation and test datasets.
They are not exact duplicate photographs.

The remaining 100 candidates are unverified.
Exhaustive manual review was stopped to prioritize
model development.

14. MODEL DEVELOPMENT DECISION

The dataset audit is sufficient to proceed with experiments,
subject to the documented limitations.

The test split must not be used for model tuning.
Further experiments and model selection should use
the validation split.

The previously completed test evaluation should be treated
as an initial evaluation, not used repeatedly for optimization.
